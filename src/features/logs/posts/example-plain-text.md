This is an example of a plain log with no images or figures, just writing. Use it as a reference for how text looks, then delete it (or keep it as a draft) once you've written your own.

## Why keep logs

Logs are short notes on what I'm working on, what went wrong, and what I learned. They don't need to be polished essays. A few paragraphs written while the details are fresh are worth more than a perfect post that never gets written.

Text can be **bold**, *italic*, or ~~struck through~~, and it can include [links](https://example.com) and `inline code`.

## Things worth writing down

- A bug that took longer than it should have, and what finally fixed it
- A paper or talk that changed how I think about something
- Notes from setting up a live production, before I forget the details

> A quote or a key takeaway can stand out as a blockquote, like this.

### A smaller heading

Headings nest down to `###` and beyond. Paragraph spacing, line length and font sizes are handled for you.
